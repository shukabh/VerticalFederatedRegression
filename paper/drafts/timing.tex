\paragraph{Release timing.} $\Rp$ observes when each release arrives, and an exact sampler that loops until it accepts takes a time that depends on the values it draws: with the plain CKS20 sampler the median time per value grows from $9.7\,\mu$s below $1\sigma$ to $25.6\,\mu$s beyond $3\sigma$. $\Op$ therefore draws the noise of every release with a fixed work budget (\textsc{SampleDGBudget}, Algorithm~\ref{alg:sub}). It is an exact rejection sampler, with a uniform-plus-geometric proposal and an acceptance test built from $\mathrm{Bernoulli}(e^{-1})$-type coins, that always runs exactly $T$ candidate trials, computes every one with the same fixed-width operations, discards the surplus and returns the first $N$ accepted values. It fails, and the release is refused, if a trial cannot be decided with the coins it drew (a bounded inner loop runs out, or a 128-bit coin equals its threshold) or if fewer than $N$ trials accept. $T$ and the inner bounds are public functions of $(\sigma_c^2, N, \xi_T)$, chosen so that the failure probability is at most $\xi_T = 2^{-80}$: a Chernoff bound on the binomial number of acceptances and a union bound over the trials. Outside a failure the output is exactly that of the unbudgeted sampler on the same coins, and the sequence of operations depends only on public parameters and on the number of accepted trials, which is independent of the values returned. We assume (W): $\Op$'s running time is a function of its sequence of operations with their public shapes and of machine noise independent of the run's secrets, not of the operand values. Under (W) the arrival times are within statistical distance $\xi_T$ per release of times that are independent of $\eta$ and of $D$, so the timing channel adds $n_{\mathrm{rel}}\xi_T$ to the slack $\gamma$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:output} (proofs, Part~A, Section~5.5, Corollary~5.21) and to the ledger's slack (composition appendix, Remark~5.9). The flooding vectors of \textsc{SanitizeBFV} are drawn the same way: their $2\alpha F$ calls per cuckoo table add $2\alpha F\xi_T$ per table to the statistical distance of G4 (Section~\ref{sec:input}), not to $\delta$, and \textsc{Setup} checks the total against $\delta_{\mathrm{psi}}$ ($2^{-75.7}$ at $10^6$ rows). At the largest $\sigma_c$ of the $10^6$-row runs ($2^{68.5}$) and $N = 2^{14}$, $T = 39{,}088 = 2.39N$; the budgeted sampler takes $0.24$\,s per release against $0.45$\,s for the plain big-integer sampler it replaces, because its limb arithmetic is vectorized, and no call failed in $1{,}000$ release-size and $300$ flooding-size calls (the smallest surplus of accepted trials was $6.9$ standard deviations). (W) is an assumption about the platform, not a theorem. It is not verified for the implementation's CPython and numpy operations, among them a lookup in a $2.9$\,kB table at value-dependent addresses, or for OpenFHE's samplers of $(u, e_0, e_1)$; on coins forced to extreme values one call's trials took the same time as on random coins to within the machine noise (medians within $6\%$). As defence in depth, $\Op$ still holds every release until a deadline $D$ after it takes up the request, fixed before the first release from public parameters and the machine (twice the worst of three timed budgeted samplings plus one re-randomized partial decryption), and refuses a release whose computation overruns it. Under (W) an overrun depends on the machine's load, not on $\eta$: it costs availability, not privacy, and is not charged.
%NEW-END

%R-BEGIN T21 algorithms.tex, the sampler subroutine
%OLD| \Function{SampleDG}{$\sigma$} exact discrete Gaussian on $\Z$ by rejection from a discrete Laplace, with big-integer
%OLD| \Statex \hspace{\algorithmicindent}arithmetic, a rational $\sigma^2$ and a CSPRNG; never by rounding a floating-point normal
%OLD| \EndFunction
%NEW-BEGIN
\Function{SampleDGBudget}{$\sigma^2,n$} $n$ exact samples of $\mathcal N_{\Z}(0,\sigma^2)$, integer $\sigma^2$, with a fixed work budget
  \State $t\gets2^b\approx\sigma$;\quad $c\gets\lceil2^{128}\sigma^2/(2t^2)\rceil/2^{128}$;\quad $(T,J_v,k_{\max})$ public functions of $(\sigma^2,n,\xi_T)$
  \For{$j=1,\dots,T$, every one with the same fixed-width operations} \Comment{128-bit coins from a CSPRNG}
    \State sign $s$;\ $u\gets\mathrm U[0,t)$;\ $v\gets\#\{1\le i\le J_v:U<e^{-i}\}$;\ $y_j\gets u+tv$ if $s=0$, else $-(u+tv+1)$
    \State $g\gets y_j^2/(2\sigma^2)-v+c\ (\ge0)$;\quad $a_j\gets\mathrm{Bern}(e^{-\lfloor g\rfloor})\wedge\mathrm{Bern}(e^{-(g-\lfloor g\rfloor)})$ \Comment{the latter with exactly $k_{\max}$ coins}
  \EndFor
  \State \textbf{fail} if a trial is undecided or $\sum_ja_j<n$;\quad \Return the first $n$ values $y_j$ with $a_j=1$ \Comment{$\Pr[\textbf{fail}]\le\xi_T$}
\EndFunction
%NEW-END
%R-END T21
